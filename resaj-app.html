Frequently Asked Questions
How do I schedule a waste pick-up?
Go to Quick Actions on the home screen and tap "Pick-up". Fill in your address, waste type, and preferred date/time. You'll receive a confirmation notification.
How do I earn green points?
You earn points by: (1) Scheduling and completing waste pick-ups, (2) Scanning QR codes at waste bins, (3) Completing quizzes and tasks, (4) Participating in community events.
How do I convert points to cash?
Go to your Wallet screen and tap "Convert Green Points". You can exchange your points for cash rewards at the current exchange rate shown in the app.
What types of waste are accepted?
We accept plastic, paper/cardboard, metal/cans, glass, organic waste, and electronics. The reward rate varies by waste type.
How do I scan a waste bin QR code?
Tap "Scan" in the bottom navigation. Point your camera at the QR code on any RE:SAJ waste bin. Scanning happens automatically and points are instantly credited.
